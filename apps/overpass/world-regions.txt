# Every region the map covers, one per line, as the URL of a Geofabrik extract.
#
# Read by bin/build-region-extract, which downloads and filters these one at a
# time and merges the results. See apps/overpass/README.md.
#
# This is europe-regions.txt plus the rest of the world, and the two are
# alternatives: point WAYSIDE_REGION_LIST at one of them, never both. A region
# renamed upstream has to be fixed in whichever list is in use, and a stale URL
# fails the build loudly rather than quietly dropping a country.
#
# Regions must not overlap, or their shared objects are imported twice. That is
# why the aggregates are left out (alps, dach, britain-and-ireland,
# united-kingdom), why this takes Hong Kong on its own rather than all of
# China, and why the United States is here as states rather than as
# us-latest.osm.pbf.
#
# A line may carry a name after the URL, which is what --refresh and the part
# files use. Without one the name is the basename, and that is ambiguous for
# anything below country level: europe/georgia and us/georgia are both
# "georgia". Subdivisions are therefore named explicitly, countries are not.
#
# Why states and provinces rather than whole countries. Peak disk is the
# largest single region, and us-latest.osm.pbf is 11 GB against France's 4.7,
# which would more than double what the filter has to hold. The nineteen states
# below are 6.4 GB together and 1.2 GB at the largest, so adding the world
# leaves the disk budget where Europe already put it. They are the states with
# a city in src/seo/cities.ts; adding another is one line.
#
# Regenerate the Europe half from Geofabrik's index, dropping the aggregates:
#   curl -s https://download.geofabrik.de/index-v1.json | \
#     jq -r '.features[].properties | select(.parent=="europe") | .urls.pbf' | sort
