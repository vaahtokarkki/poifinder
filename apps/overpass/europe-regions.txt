# The regions that together make up europe-latest.osm.pbf, one URL per line.
#
# Read by bin/build-region-extract, which downloads and filters these one at a
# time instead of the 35 GB continent file. See apps/overpass/README.md.
#
# Geofabrik lists 53 children under europe. Four of them are aggregates that
# overlap the rest and are left out here, or their objects would be imported
# twice: alps, dach (Germany/Austria/Switzerland), britain-and-ireland, and
# united-kingdom (great-britain plus Northern Ireland, both of which are
# already covered by great-britain and ireland-and-northern-ireland).
#
# Russia is not in this list because it is not in europe-latest either:
# Geofabrik files it as a continent of its own. Adding russia-latest.osm.pbf
# here is all it would take.
#
# Any OSM extract works, from Geofabrik or anywhere else. Order does not
# matter: osmium merge sorts the pieces back together at the end.
#
# Regenerate from Geofabrik's index, dropping the four aggregates by hand:
#   curl -s https://download.geofabrik.de/index-v1.json | \
#     jq -r '.features[].properties | select(.parent=="europe") | .urls.pbf' | sort
