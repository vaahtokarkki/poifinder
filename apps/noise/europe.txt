# Geofabrik extracts covering every tier 1 European city in cities.json.
#
# Used as:
#
#   NOISE_EXTRACTS="$(grep -v '^#' europe.txt | tr '\n' ' ')"
#
# One extract per country rather than europe-latest.osm.pbf, for the same
# reason apps/overpass assembles a region at a time: the continent file is a
# single download of 30 GB and more, and osmium reading it costs the whole of
# it on the disk, where these are the same places one country at a time with
# nothing ever held but the largest of them.
#
# Sizes are approximate and grow. The total here is roughly 20 GB downloaded,
# cached after the first run, and it covers 21 of the 36 tier 1 cities.
#
# The other 15 are outside Europe — Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand and
# the United States — and want their own list. The US in particular is worth
# taking state by state (us-northeast, us-west, ...) rather than as
# us-latest.osm.pbf, which is 11 GB on its own.
