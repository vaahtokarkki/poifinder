# wayside.cc
# Every indexable page is prerendered and listed in the sitemap.

User-agent: *
Allow: /

# Shared links are one view of the map with coordinates in the query string.
# They are not pages, and they are not blocked here on purpose: every one of
# them carries a canonical back to the map root, which folds them into it.
# Disallowing them instead would stop a crawler ever reading that canonical.


# Two AI crawlers are turned away below, and the line between them and the
# ones still allowed is what the crawl is *for*, not who runs it.
#
# A crawler that answers a question someone is asking right now cites where
# the answer came from, and that citation is a person arriving on the site.
# That is the same trade as a search index, so those agents keep their access:
# GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User, Googlebot and GoogleOther, Applebot.
#
# A crawler gathering a training corpus sends nothing back. Over the week of
# 13-20 Aug 2026 meta-externalagent alone made 7,346 requests, 32% of all
# traffic to the zone and roughly thirty times the number of human requests
# in the same period, and produced no referrals. ClaudeBot's 1,199 are the
# same shape at smaller scale. Neither is malicious and both honour robots;
# they are simply taking a copy and giving no reason to host it.
#
# These are the training tokens only. Anthropic's user-facing agents,
# Claude-User and Claude-SearchBot, fetch pages because a person asked a
# question, so they fall on the allowed side with OAI-SearchBot and stay
# under the wildcard above. Meta's meta-externalfetch is likewise the
# link-preview agent, not the corpus crawler.
#
# Both tokens are matched by prefix and neither collides with an allowed
# agent, so no ordering games are needed: a crawler that matches a named
# group here ignores the "User-agent: *" group entirely.

User-agent: meta-externalagent
Disallow: /

User-agent: ClaudeBot
Disallow: /

Sitemap: https://wayside.cc/sitemap.xml
